# Archive Services Agreement

Between **Kontor Hanseatic GmbH** ("the Client"), Lange Reihe 7, 20099 Hamburg,
and **Northwind Traders GmbH** ("the Contractor"), Rosenstraße 14, 10178 Berlin.

Dated 14 September 2026. Page 1 of 3.

## 1. Scope

The Contractor shall digitise the Client's paper archive as listed in the
inventory of 30 September 2026: fourteen boxes, approximately 5,334 sheets.

## 2. Standard

Pages are scanned at 300 dpi, deskewed, and delivered as searchable PDF/A.
Any sheet the Contractor cannot read is reported rather than silently omitted.

## 3. Custody

Boxes remain the property of the Client throughout and are held in a locked
room to which only named staff of the Contractor have access.
